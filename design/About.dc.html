About Foodsera

Nigerian food, the way we cook it at home

Foodsera is a home for Nigerian recipes that actually work: tested, written step by step, and explained so you can cook them in Lagos, London or anywhere your kitchen is.

The story behind Foodsera

I learned to cook standing beside the women in my family, watching more than measuring. A handful of this, a derica of that, “you will know when it is ready.” The food was wonderful. Writing it down was another matter.

When friends moved abroad, the messages started. How much egusi for a small pot? What can I use instead of ugu? Why does my jollof never taste like party jollof? Foodsera began as my answers to those questions.
