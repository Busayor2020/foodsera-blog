faqs:
  - q: Can I use basmati rice?
    a: Yes, but use about a third less stock and check it after 20 minutes. Basmati cooks faster and turns soft if it has too much liquid.
  - q: Why is my jollof rice soggy?
    a: Usually too much stock or not enough reducing of the pepper base. Reduce the blend well and add only enough stock to just cover the rice.
  - q: How do I get the smoky party taste at home?
    a: Let the rice at the bottom of the pot catch for the last few minutes on higher heat, or add a pinch of smoked paprika with the spices.

No Nigerian party is complete without a big pot of jollof, and this is the one people ask about: deep red, every grain separate, with a gentle smoke from the bottom of the pot.

The secret is a well-reduced pepper base and a lid you leave alone. Let the steam do the work, and give the bottom a few minutes to catch at the end.

It goes with almost everything, but for a full party spread serve it next to a pot of [egusi soup](/recipes/egusi-soup) and a tray of [puff-puff](/recipes/puff-puff).
