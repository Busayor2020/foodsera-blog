Zobo is deep red, tart and full of ginger, and no Nigerian party is complete without a cold jug of it. It's made from dried hibiscus, simmered with pineapple and spice.

It takes half an hour to make and a couple of hours to chill. Make it the day before and it tastes even better.

Pour a glass alongside a bowl of warm [puff-puff](/recipes/puff-puff).
