Akara is breakfast on the streets of every Nigerian city: crisp outside, fluffy inside and best eaten straight from the pan. In the north it goes by kosai.

Peeled bean flour makes it a 25-minute job. The secret is in the beating, so give the batter a good few minutes with your spoon.

Made with the same beans as [moi moi](/recipes/moi-moi), and just as good with a cup of pap.
