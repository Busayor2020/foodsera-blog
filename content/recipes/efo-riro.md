Ẹ̀fọ́ riro means "stirred greens" in Yoruba, and it's the stew that gets a Sunday table talking. Deep green leaves in a rich, red, peppery base with plenty of meat and fish.

It comes together quickly once the pepper base is ready. Squeeze the greens well and add them last, so they stay bright.

Serve it with pounded yam, or next to [egusi soup](/recipes/egusi-soup) for a proper Yoruba feast.
