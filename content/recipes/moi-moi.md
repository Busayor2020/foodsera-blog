Moi moi is what makes a party plate feel complete. Silky, peppery and steamed until just set, with a surprise of egg and fish in the middle.

The work is all in the beans. Peel them well and blend until the batter is completely smooth, and the steamer does the rest.

Serve it next to [smoky party jollof](/recipes/jollof-rice), or on its own with a cup of pap for breakfast.
