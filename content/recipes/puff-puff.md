faqs:
  - q: Why is my puff-puff flat?
    a: The batter was too thin, or it didn't rise long enough. It should be thick, stretchy and doubled in size before frying.
  - q: Can I make puff-puff ahead?
    a: "They are best warm, but you can make the batter up to 2 hours ahead and keep it covered. Fried puff-puff reheats well in a hot oven for 5 minutes."
  - q: Why is my puff-puff raw in the middle?
    a: The oil was too hot, so the outside browned before the middle cooked. Lower the heat a little and fry for longer.

Puff-puff is the first thing to disappear from any small chops tray. Soft and airy inside, golden outside, and just sweet enough to keep you reaching for one more.

The secret is a thick, well-risen batter and oil that isn't too hot. Get those two right and they turn themselves over in the pot.
