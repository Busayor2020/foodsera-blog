Pepper soup is what Nigerians reach for on a cold evening, after a long week, or when they're feeling under the weather. Light, fiery and full of flavour.

Fresh catfish is the classic in the Niger Delta. It cooks in minutes, so the whole pot is on the table in half an hour.

Have it on its own, or as a starter before a plate of [jollof rice](/recipes/jollof-rice).
